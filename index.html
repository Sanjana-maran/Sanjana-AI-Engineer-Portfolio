PROJECT 01 · KNOWLEDGE SYSTEMS⌘

Zeb Wiki

An agent-based knowledge workflow that turns scattered project documents into a structured, searchable wiki, inspired by Andrej Karpathy’s “LLM Wiki” concept.

LangChain OrchestratorAgentCoreClaude Sonnet 4.5PostgreSQLAWS S3Human-in-the-loop
↗ 90% less time locating past project documentation, as reported in the resume.
Explore problem, architecture & my role

01 · Problem

Project knowledge was spread across Teams and SharePoint documents. Finding scope, technologies, project context, or similar past work required manually reviewing multiple files, making knowledge reuse slow.

02 · Solution & architecture

Built an agent-based wiki pipeline inspired by Andrej Karpathy’s LLM Wiki idea: turn source documents into structured entities and persistent Markdown knowledge pages, then make them discoverable through semantic retrieval.

Teams / SharePoint docs→LangChain Orchestrator Agent→AgentCore Executor→Entity extraction→Similarity / duplicate check→Wiki read / write→S3 + PostgreSQL embeddings
  • Entity extraction: identify projects, clients, technology stacks, scope, and other attributes.
  • Wiki management: create or update Markdown pages and maintain the schema as new entity types appear.
  • Semantic retrieval: store wiki Markdown in AWS S3 and embeddings in PostgreSQL for similar-project discovery.
  • Human-in-the-loop: include a human review checkpoint for ambiguous or low-confidence extracted details and proposed knowledge updates before they are accepted.

Reference: Inspired by Andrej Karpathy’s “LLM Wiki” concept, which treats a knowledge base as a maintained, evolving collection of linked notes rather than a one-off document summary.

03 · My role

  • Designed and implemented the agentic workflow for transforming source documents into structured wiki knowledge.
  • Built the LangChain orchestration flow and connected document processing, entity extraction, similarity detection, and wiki management tools.
  • Implemented S3 storage for Markdown pages and PostgreSQL embeddings for semantic search.
  • Worked on dynamic entity/schema management and integrated Claude Sonnet 4.5 for document understanding and extraction.
  • Evaluated extraction quality and identified agent execution cost as an optimization area.

04 · Results

Created a searchable organizational knowledge base, enabled discovery of similar projects, and reduced time spent locating previous project documentation by 90% (as stated in the resume). The work also identified cost-efficiency opportunities for production-scale use.

05 · Tech stack

Python · LangChain · AgentCore · Claude Sonnet 4.5 · AWS S3 · PostgreSQL · Embeddings · RAG / vector search · Markdown

PROJECT 02 · LEGAL AI§

NFI Redlining

RAG-based contract review proof of concept that compares vendor/customer contract clauses against company policies and suggests corrections in Microsoft Word.

PythonGPTRAGLangChainPostgreSQLFastAPI
↗ Made policy discrepancies easier to identify and review in-document.
Explore problem, architecture & my role

01 · Problem

Legal teams needed a more efficient way to identify discrepancies between vendor/customer contracts and internal company policies.

02 · Solution & architecture

Processed documents submitted through a chatbot/API, chunked the contract text, retrieved relevant policy passages from PostgreSQL using semantic similarity, and sent the contract chunks plus retrieved context to GPT for analysis.

Document via chatbot / API→Ingestion & chunking→PostgreSQL policy search→RAG context + GPT→Word Track Changes & comments

Potential discrepancies were shown directly in the Word document by striking through flagged text and adding suggested corrections as comments for legal review.

03 · My role

  • Developed the document-processing workflow, chunking, similarity search, and LLM analysis.
  • Implemented the RAG pipeline over company policy content stored in PostgreSQL.
  • Integrated GPT-based clause comparison and Microsoft Word Track Changes/comment automation.
  • Helped connect the workflow to chatbot/API-based document submission.

04 · Results

Delivered a working POC that helped surface policy deviations faster and placed proposed corrections in the document for legal teams to review, reducing manual review effort.

05 · Tech stack

Python · GPT · RAG · LangChain · PostgreSQL / vector search · FastAPI / REST API · NLP · Microsoft Word automation

Precisely Template Migration

Explored LLM-assisted conversion of OpenText Exstream XML templates to Precisely RapidCX, evaluating output accuracy and functional/visual fidelity.

GenAIClaudeAmazon Nova ProXMLAWS
↗ POC targeted 85% automated conversion coverage with minimal manual adjustments.
Explore problem, architecture & my role

01 · Problem

Moving legacy OpenText Exstream templates to Precisely RapidCX can be complex and manual, particularly when templates vary in structure and complexity.

02 · Solution & architecture

Parsed and analyzed source XML, identified reusable patterns, and used prompt-engineered LLM workflows to generate corresponding target structures. Tested across template complexity levels and compared outputs against originals.

Exstream XML→Parse & inspect structure→Prompt / LLM conversion→RapidCX target structure→Accuracy & fidelity evaluation

03 · My role

  • Analyzed template structure and identified reusable conversion patterns.
  • Designed and refined prompts based on conversion test results.
  • Compared Claude and Amazon Nova Pro outputs.
  • Evaluated accuracy, visual fidelity, functional equivalence, and limitations across complexity levels.

04 · Results

Demonstrated technical feasibility, documented conversion patterns and failure cases, and established evaluation considerations. The POC targeted 85% conversion coverage with minimal manual adjustments; this is a target, not a claimed measured result.
